About

Who

Ross Imlach. Born and raised in Scotland.

I work across cinematography, live production and platform engineering. That means features, commercials, short films and music videos behind the camera; lighting, video, design and production for live shows; and the tools and systems that sit behind the work.

More of my work now moves between these disciplines. I like being involved in both the creative decisions and the practical systems that make them possible.

I also keep a notebook about the things I’m building and figuring out. Colour and sound work lives over at The Studio, which is still taking shape.
